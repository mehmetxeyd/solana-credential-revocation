Credential revocation on Solana

Issue a signed credential, then revoke it on-chain. Anyone can check its status without a wallet and without trusting a server.

Wallet not connected

Your connected wallet becomes the issuer. Signing happens in Phantom and costs nothing.

No wallet needed. This checks the issuer’s signature and then looks for a revocation record on Solana devnet.

Only the issuer’s wallet can revoke. This sends one devnet transaction, which needs a small amount of devnet SOL.

Program on devnet. Revocation records are PDAs derived from the issuer and the SHA-256 hash of the credential.
